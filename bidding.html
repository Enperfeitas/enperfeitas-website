      Before you bid

      • Bidding is by invitation only.
      • A binding bid means you cannot take it back while bidding is open. Your right of withdrawal after purchase still applies.
      • All amounts are whole Swedish kronor (SEK) and include moms.
      • A bid must be at least the minimum shown: the opening bid, or the current high bid plus the step.
      • If a bid arrives in the last 5 minutes, the closing time moves to that many minutes after the bid, so everyone has time to answer.
      • The winner is invoiced within 24 hours and pays within 48 hours. If payment does not arrive, the piece is offered to the next bidder.
      • Only your first name is shown next to your bids.
      • Shipping is calculated by location and added to the invoice after bidding closes. Buyers in Stockholm can collect the piece from the studio in Vällingby instead.
      • The piece is dispatched within 7 days of payment.
      • You have the right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving the piece. Tell us by email at info@enperfeitas.com within that time. The piece must be returned in essentially the same condition it arrived in. Return shipping is paid by the buyer. The amount you paid is refunded within 14 days of your message.
