Refunds and Returns

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Your right to change your mind: 14 days

If you are a consumer and buy from us online, you have 14 days to withdraw from your purchase without giving a reason. For goods, the 14 days start the day you receive the goods (or the last item, if you ordered several in one order).

To withdraw, tell us clearly by email to info@enperfeitas.com, with your name and order details. You can use the form at the bottom of this page, but you do not have to. Send the goods back within 14 days of telling us.

Returning goods

You pay the cost of sending the goods back. Please pack them well. You may open and try the goods as you would in a shop, but you can be asked to cover any loss in value caused by handling beyond that. Please return them in a condition close to how you received them.

Your refund

We refund the price you paid, including the standard delivery cost you paid on the original order, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal message. We may wait until we have the goods back or you have shown that you sent them. The refund goes to the payment method you used. If you chose a more expensive delivery than our standard option, the extra cost is not refunded.

What cannot be returned

  • Custom-made or personalised items, such as bespoke books and boxes made to your specifications. These are exempt from the right of withdrawal. Your rights if something is wrong with the work are covered under Faulty or damaged items below.
  • Digital downloads (PDF and video guides) once delivery has started, when you have agreed at checkout to immediate delivery and accepted that this ends your right of withdrawal. If you did not give that agreement, the 14 days apply as usual.
  • Workshops on a fixed date are leisure activities booked for a specific date, so the 14-day right does not apply. Instead, our cancellation terms apply: see the workshop pages.

Faulty or damaged items

If an item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, contact us at info@enperfeitas.com as soon as you notice, with your name, order details and, if you can, a photo. You have three years to make a complaint, and a complaint made within two months of discovering the fault is always in time. We will repair or replace the item, or reduce the price or refund you if that is not possible. We pay the cost of returning a faulty item.

Workshops and memberships

Workshop cancellation and refund terms are shown on each workshop page. For studio memberships, if you withdraw within 14 days of buying and have already used the studio, we refund the part you have not used.

Disputes

If we cannot agree, you can take the matter to the Swedish National Board for Consumer Disputes (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden), arn.se. Consumer advice is available from Konsumentverket and the municipal consumer advisers.
