Enperfeitas bidding guide

Starting a new round

Everything you do between one round and the next, in order: what to change in the Sheet, what to publish on the website, and which menu item to use when. Keep this page open next to the Sheet.

The short version
  1. Invitations > Start new round: pauses last round's people and empties the bids.
  2. Change the Config tab to the new piece (the table in Step 2).
  3. Add the new photos to the website and publish.
  4. Approve new sign-ups and Generate codes.
  5. Ask previous participants, then nudge the ones who have not answered.
  6. Test in test mode, then set test_mode to FALSE and status to open.
  7. Send invitations. During bidding, Send reminders.
  8. After closing: winner email, payment details, dispatch email.

Before you begin

Do not start a new round until the old one is completely finished.

  • The auction is closed, and the winner has been emailed, has paid, and the piece has been dispatched or collected.
  • You do not need anything from the old Bids tab any more. (It is saved for you in a backup tab in Step 1, but the Bids tab itself is emptied.)
  • You have the new piece's name, photos, description, opening bid and the dates.
Why this order matters

The winner emails find the winner from the Bids tab. Start new round empties that tab. If you start the new round first, "Send winner email" will say there are no bids.

This is only a suggestion, based on how your first round ran. Adjust the days to suit you.

Email limit

A free Gmail account can send about 100 emails a day. The question email, the invitation and each reminder all count. The script checks before it sends and stops safely if the limit is too low, and you can finish the next day. With a big list, spread the steps over different days.

1

Start the new round

In the Sheet, choose Invitations > Start new round.

It only works once the auction is closed. Before it changes anything, it shows you a summary:

  • Paused until they answer: the people who took part last round (approved, and an invitation was sent), plus anyone who answered "not this round" before. Their status becomes returning.
  • Not touched: new sign-ups waiting for approval, people you approved but have not invited yet, blocked people, and people who removed themselves.

Press YES to go on, or NO to stop with nothing changed.

What it does when you press YES
  • Copies the Invitees tab to Invitees backup (date), and the finished round's bids to Bids (date) (piece title).
  • Empties the Bids tab.
  • For the paused people only: sets status to returning and empties the columns that belong to one round (sent, reminded, asked, answered, delivery note, payment requested, paid, dispatched).
  • Clears winner_emailed in Config and writes round_started.

It does not change the piece, the closing time, the status, the photos or any other Config value. You do that next.

If you press it twice

The second time you will see a warning, because anyone invited since the first time would be paused too. Only continue if you are sure.

2

Update the Config tab

Open the Config tab. Column A is the name of each setting (do not rename it). Change the values in column B.

SettingChange it?What to write
piece_titleEvery roundThe name of the new piece. It appears on the page, in every email subject and in the emails.
opening_bidUsuallyWhole SEK, moms included. The first bid must be at least this.
min_stepRarelyHow much higher each bid must be than the last. 100 is the default.
start_timeOptionalWhen bidding opens, for example 2026-12-05T18:00:00+01:00. Before this time the page shows the piece and a countdown, with no bid form. Leave empty to open at once. It must be earlier than closing_time.
closing_timeEvery roundWhen bidding ends, written like 2026-12-06T20:00:00+01:00. The last part is the Swedish time offset: +01:00 from the last Sunday of October to the last Sunday of March, and +02:00 in summer. A bid in the last minutes moves it automatically, so do not edit it while bidding runs.
extension_minutesRarely5 by default. The page tells bidders about it in the rules.

winner_emailed and round_started are filled in and cleared by the script. Leave them alone.

Tip: if a time cell turns into a date by itself, select it, choose Format > Number > Plain text, and type it again.

3

Add the photos to the website

  1. Save each photo as .webp, about 1200 pixels on the long side and under 300 KB. Portrait photos (4 to 5) look best.
  2. Name them with the piece name first, then a number: blue-book-1.webp, blue-book-2.webp. Number 1 is the main photo.
  3. Put them in the website folder images/bidding.
  4. Commit and publish them in GitHub Desktop like any other change.

The new photo_prefix in Config must match the file names exactly. If a photo cannot be found, the page simply hides the photo area.

4

Update the sign-up form

  • In the Google Form, update the description with the new dates (when the list closes, when invitations go out, when bidding takes place). If you do not know them yet, keep the wording that says the next round's date is not set.
  • Make sure the Form is accepting responses until the day you want the list to close. Then turn off "Accepting responses" in the Form's Responses tab.
5

Approve new sign-ups and make codes

People who signed up for the new round appear in the Invitees tab with an empty or pending status. They were not touched by Step 1.

  1. In the status column, write approved for everyone you want to invite. Leave the others as they are.
  2. Choose Invitations > Generate codes. Each approved person without a code gets one. It skips rows with no first name or email and tells you.

Do not write approved next to people who took part last round. They are returning and become approved by themselves when they answer yes (Step 6).

6

Ask last round's people

Choose Invitations > Ask previous participants. It shows how many people are waiting for an answer, how many have not been asked yet, and how many were asked but have not answered.

  • YES asks those who have not been asked yet.
  • NO sends a gentle nudge to those who were asked and have not answered. Use it a few days later.
  • CANCEL sends nothing.

The email has a personal link to the answer page. The person chooses one of three answers, and the Sheet changes straight away:

Their choiceStatus becomesWhat it means
Yes, include meapprovedThey get this round's invitation when you use Send invitations.
Not this rounddeclinedNo invitation or reminder this round. They are asked again next round.
Remove me from the listunsubscribedNever asked or emailed again.
(no answer)returningNothing is sent to them about this round. No invitation, no reminder.

The time the question was sent is in the asked column and the time they answered is in answered. You can change a status by hand at any time.

The answer page must be online

The link points to join-round.html on your website, so publish the website folder (GitHub Desktop) before you send this email.

7

Test everything

Set test_mode to TRUE. In test mode every email goes only to you, marked [TEST], and nothing is written in the Sheet's "sent", "asked" or "reminded" columns.

  • Run Send invitations. Check the email that reaches you: the piece name, dates, opening bid and the button.
  • Open the bidding page from that button. Check the photos, description, details and countdown.
  • Run Ask previous participants and look at the question email.
  • Place a test bid with your own code, then choose Reset test bids (it only works in test mode).
  • After testing, check the closing_time and status in Config again, because a test can move the closing time or close the auction.
Careful with other people's links

Test mode protects the emails. It does not protect the pages. If you place a bid, or press an answer button, using another person's personal link, it is saved for that person, even in test mode. To test the pages safely, add a row for yourself in the Invitees tab (your own email, approved, Generate codes, and a date in the sent cell before Step 1, so it becomes returning like the others). Use your own link, and delete the row when you are done.

8

Go live and invite

  1. In Config set test_mode to FALSE.
  2. Set status to open. With a future start_time the page shows the piece with a countdown, and bidding opens by itself at that time.
  3. Check closing_time once more, including the +01:00 or +02:00.
  4. Choose Invitations > Send invitations. It emails every approved person who has a code and no sent time: your new sign-ups, and the returning people who said yes. You see how many before you confirm.

The invitation says what happens after winning: the winner is emailed after bidding closes and replies with shipping or collection, the payment details follow, and payment is due within 48 hours of receiving them.

9

While bidding is open

  • Send reminders: YES sends the reminder only to people with no bid yet, NO sends it to everyone invited. A reminded column records who got one.
  • Stop one person from bidding: change their status to revoked.
  • Cancel a single bid: in the Bids tab, write TRUE in the void column of that row.
  • Close early: Close auction. Otherwise it closes by itself at closing_time.
10

After bidding closes

  1. Send winner email: shows you the winner and the winning bid, and after you confirm it congratulates them and asks whether they want the piece shipped or collected. It has no payment link.
  2. When they reply, Send payment details. Choose YES for shipped (then type the shipping cost) or NO for collection. The email has your payment link, or the no-shipping link for collection.
  3. When the payment arrives, type the date in the paid column of the winner's row in the Invitees tab.
  4. Send dispatch email when the piece is posted. You can add the tracking number. It warns you if the paid cell is empty.
If the winner does not pay in time

In the Bids tab write TRUE in the void column of every row from that person (their earlier, lower bids would otherwise become the top bid). Delete the winner_emailed row in Config. Then Send winner email goes to the next highest bidder.

When all of this is finished, you are ready to start the next round at Step 1.

Quick reference

The status words

StatusMeaning
pending (or empty)New sign-up waiting for you to approve.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting status. After Start new round it is still closed, so the page shows the piece as closed. Set it to open in Step 8.
  • Forgetting test_mode. With TRUE, "Send invitations" only sends to you. With FALSE, it sends to everyone. Check it before every real send.
  • A wrong time offset. +01:00 in winter, +02:00 in summer. A wrong one moves the closing time by an hour.
  • A closing time in the past. The page treats the round as closed, whatever status says.
  • Starting the new round too early. Do it only after the winner has been emailed and has paid (see "Before you begin").
  • Photo names that do not match. The file names and photo_prefix must be identical, lowercase, with hyphens.
  • Not publishing the website. The photos and the answer page only appear once you commit and publish the website folder.
  • Not publishing the script. After changing Code.gs, use Deploy > Manage deployments > New version. Saving alone does not update it.

If something looks wrong, the message in the Sheet says what happened and nothing more is done after that point. Every menu item asks you to confirm before it sends or changes anything.
