Delivery times, destinations and costs are on the Shipping Info page. Digital guides are sent to you by email after purchase.

Consumers have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase without giving a reason. Everything you need to know, including how to withdraw and a withdrawal form, is on the Refunds and Returns page.

Goods must match what we have described. If something is faulty, you have three years to make a complaint, and a complaint made within two months of finding the fault is always in time. See Refunds and Returns for how to contact us and what we do.
